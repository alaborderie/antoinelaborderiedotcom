The home page loads the PostHog analytics script, published by PostHog Inc., to measure its traffic. The data is hosted in the European Union (PostHog Cloud EU).

PostHog is configured without cookies: it writes nothing to browser storage. Each page view sends the page address and title, the referring site, the browser (with its user-agent string) and its language, the operating system, the device type, the screen and window size, and the time zone. When you leave the page, it also sends the time spent on it and how far it was scrolled.

Visitors are counted on PostHog's servers by hashing the IP address and browser with a salt that changes every day. The same visitor is therefore not recognised from one day to the next. The IP address is used only for this calculation and is not stored.

These traffic statistics rely on the publisher's legitimate interest and are kept for 13 months. You can object to this measurement and exercise your rights by writing to the contact email above, and you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

The site's code configures no custom tracking events or advertising trackers. Interaction autocapture, heatmaps and session recording are turned off.
